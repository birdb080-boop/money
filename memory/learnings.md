## HELOC funnel — conversion playbook (researched 2026-06)
- **Interactive quiz/estimator beats static forms by a lot.** Static mortgage landing
  pages convert ~1–2% of visitors; the best-built ones hit 8%+; **interactive quizzes/
  calculators routinely hit 40%+.** Our funnel leads with the equity estimator for this reason.
- **HELOC-specific:** a calculator that shows a borrowing estimate from home value −
  balance measurably lifts conversion. (We show "up to $X," clearly disclaimed.)
- **Friction kills completion.** Ask 3–5 fields, one question per screen, enable autofill
  (`autocomplete` tokens), mobile-first. We did all four.
- **Speed-to-lead is the #1 funded-loan driver** — minutes beat hours. The funnel alerts
  Martin instantly (Zapier action B); Martin follows up fast. JARVIS only drafts.
- **Above-the-fold trust** (headshot/team photo, license badge, "no credit pull") raises
  form starts. Added Meet Martin + NMLS badge + security line.
- **Exit-intent recovery + sticky mobile CTA** reclaim abandoners — both added.
- **Meta Pixel `Lead` event** lets the ad algorithm optimize toward submitters → cheaper
  qualified leads over time. Housing ads = Meta "Special Ad Category" (no age/gender/ZIP
  targeting) + privacy-policy URL required.
- **Compliance that protects the license:** no rates/APRs (dodges TILA trigger-term
  disclosure rules), Equal Housing, NMLS + Consumer Access link, TCPA consent w/ STOP/HELP,
  privacy + terms pages. Never fabricate testimonials (UDAAP risk).
