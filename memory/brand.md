## The "lux" look (this is the style I liked — keep it)
**Vibe:** Arizona desert luxury at golden hour. Calm, confident, high-end but warm.

### Colors
- Background: warm **cream / ivory / sand** (#F5EFE6-ish), soft gradients
- Accent: **gold / bronze / tan** (#B08D57 / #C9A227 range) — for script + rules + icons
- Anchor: **charcoal / near-black** (#1A1A1A) — footers, logo, headline serif
- Keep it monochrome-warm. No bright/cold colors. Black VIP logo only.

### Typography
- **Headline:** bold high-contrast **serif** (Playfair Display vibe), large, dark
- **Accent word:** elegant **gold script** (e.g. *Real Results*, *reset*, *para recargar*)
  — italic, flowing, used on ONE key word per piece
- **Labels/taglines:** small **uppercase sans**, wide letter-spacing, muted

### Imagery
- Saguaro cactus, modern desert luxury home, warm golden-hour light, mountains
- Soft, airy, lots of negative space

### Motifs
- Thin gold horizontal rules · small **heart** dividers · circular gold badges
  ("Another Happy Client") · rounded soft-shadow cards for reviews/lists
- Icon rows: Local Experts · Smart Strategy · Seamless Experience · Clients First
