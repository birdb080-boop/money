## 🎨 Master style block (paste at top of every prompt)
> Vertical 4:5 (or 9:16 story) social graphic. **Desert-luxury** aesthetic: warm cream/
> ivory/sand background, gold & bronze accents, charcoal near-black text. Editorial,
> premium, calm — lots of negative space. Background photo: Arizona saguaro cactus +
> a modern luxury desert home at golden hour, soft warm light. Typography: large bold
> high-contrast **serif** headline; ONE accent word in elegant **gold script**; small
> uppercase wide-letter-spaced taglines; thin gold rule lines and a small heart divider.
> Leave clean empty space at the bottom for a logo and a fine-print compliance line.
> No watermarks, no placeholder text, no fake handles. High-end real-estate brand feel.
