# HELOC Lead Follow-Up Templates (EN + ES)

> **You send these — JARVIS never auto-sends to a borrower (Directive Zero).** Copy, swap
> `{{first_name}}`, and fire from your phone/email. Built in your voice (MARTY: move first,
> ask surgical questions, reframe, 2–3 options, yes/no next step). **Compliance:** no rates,
> APRs, payments, or approval promises. Use "subject to underwriting" language. On any SMS
> marketing blast append "Reply STOP to opt out" — not needed on 1:1 replies to a lead who
> just asked you to contact them.
>
> **#1 rule: speed.** Text within 5 minutes of the lead landing. Minutes beat hours.
