The Simple Version

Equity, made simple.

Your home equity is the part of your home you actually own — its value minus what you still owe. A home equity line of credit (HELOC) lets qualified homeowners borrow against that equity and pay interest only on what they use.

Good Questions

Before you ask, we've got you.

Will this affect my credit score?
No. The estimator and your initial review involve no credit pull. If you decide to move forward later, we'll always tell you before any credit check happens.
How much can I actually borrow?
It depends on your home's value, your remaining mortgage balance, your credit, and income. Many programs allow qualified homeowners to access a large share of their equity, but the exact amount is determined during underwriting. Your free review gives you a personalized number.
What's the difference between a HELOC and a cash-out refinance?
A HELOC is a line of credit you draw from as needed, paying interest only on what you use. A cash-out refinance replaces your existing mortgage with a larger one and gives you the difference in cash. Martin will walk you through which fits your situation.

Is there any cost or obligation?
The equity review is free, and there's no obligation. We'll show you your options clearly. If it's not the right move for you, we'll tell you that too.
