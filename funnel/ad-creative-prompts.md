# HELOC Ad Creative — image prompts (desert-lux) + on-image text

> Paste these into your image tool (ChatGPT image / Canva photo). They pair with the
> ad text in `ad-copy.md`. **You approve every creative before it runs** (Directive Zero).
> After generating: drop on the **Team Miranda logo** + the **fine-print line** (bottom).
> **Never put a rate, APR, or payment on the image.**

## 🎨 Master style block (paste at the top of every prompt)
> Vertical 4:5 (feed) or 9:16 (story) ad. **Desert-luxury** aesthetic: warm cream/ivory/sand
> background, gold &amp; bronze accents, charcoal near-black text. Editorial, premium, calm —
> lots of negative space. Background: a modern Arizona desert home at golden hour with a
> saguaro and soft warm light. Typography: large bold high-contrast **serif** headline; ONE
> accent word in elegant **gold script**; small uppercase wide-letter-spaced tagline; a thin
> gold rule line. Leave clean empty space at the bottom for a logo and a fine-print line.
> No rates, no fake handles, no watermarks. High-end real-estate brand feel.
