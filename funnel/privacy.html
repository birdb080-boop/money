Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Martin Miranda and V.I.P. Mortgage, Inc. ("we," "us," "Team Miranda") collect, use, and share information when you use this website or request a home-equity review. By using this site, you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, ZIP code, and the estimates you enter about your home (such as approximate value, mortgage balance, credit range, and how you'd like to use the funds).
  • Information collected automatically: device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, and similar usage data, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Advertising identifiers: if you arrive from an ad, we may receive a click identifier (for example, a Facebook fbclid) to measure ad performance.
We do not ask for, and you should never submit, sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, full account numbers, or income documents through this website's form. The equity estimator is illustrative only and is not a credit decision.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and provide a personalized equity review and mortgage options;
  • To contact you about your inquiry by phone, text, and email with your consent (see our SMS & Terms);
  • To operate, secure, and improve this site; and
  • To measure and improve our advertising.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to serve you and operate our business:

  • Service providers that help us run the site, store leads, and follow up (for example, hosting, form, and CRM providers), under confidentiality obligations;
  • Lenders, investors, and partners as necessary to evaluate and process a loan request you initiate;
  • As required by law or to protect rights, safety, and against fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising & analytics

We may use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site and to measure ad performance. These tools may set cookies and record that an action (such as submitting the form) occurred; we do not send your name, phone, or email to these advertising tools. You can opt out of personalized ads at aboutads.info/choices and manage Google Analytics with the opt-out add-on.

Your choices

  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help at any time.
  • Cookies: adjust your browser settings to limit or block cookies.

California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request access or deletion, and to opt out of certain sharing, subject to legal exceptions. We do not sell personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Data security & retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, and retain it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children

This site is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date reflects the most recent changes.
