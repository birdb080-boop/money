## Do NOT (compliance guardrails)
- ❌ No interest rates, APRs, payment amounts, or "as low as…" (triggers TILA disclosure rules).
- ❌ No "guaranteed," "pre-approved," "you qualify," or urgency/scarcity that isn't true.
- ❌ No targeting by age, gender, or ZIP radius (Special Ad Category forbids it).
- ❌ No fake testimonials or fake countdowns.
