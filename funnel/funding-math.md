# The $5M Plan — what it takes (campaign math)

> **Planning estimates, not promises.** Real numbers depend on your market, credit mix,
> ad creative, and — most of all — **how fast you follow up.** Tune the assumptions to
> your own results after week 1. Nothing here is a guarantee of funding or approval.

## The target
**$5,000,000 in funded HELOC / cash-out over ~8 weeks.**

The lever you control most is **average funded amount**. The bigger each deal, the fewer you need:

| Avg funded cash-out | Funded loans needed | Per week (8 wks) |
|---|---|---|
| $75,000 | ~67 | ~8–9 |
| $100,000 | **50** | ~6–7 |
| $150,000 | ~34 | ~4–5 |
| $250,000 | ~20 | ~2–3 |

## Working backward to leads (the funnel)
Using mid-range industry benchmarks (interactive quiz funnel like yours converts far
better than a static page). Example below assumes **$100k avg → 50 funded loans:**

| Stage | Assumed rate | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Funded loans | — | **50** |
| Applications started | ~40% app→funded | ~125 |
| Leads (form submits) | ~35% lead→app | ~360 |
| Funnel visitors (ad clicks) | ~15% visitor→lead* | ~2,400 |
| Ad impressions | ~1.5% CTR | ~160,000 |

\* Your interactive estimator should beat 15%; static mortgage pages sit at 1–2%, quizzes
often hit 30–40%. If you convert visitors at 30%, you need **half** the clicks.

## Ad budget ballpark
At a typical mortgage/finance Facebook CPC of **~$2–$5**:

| Visitors needed | @ $2 CPC | @ $5 CPC |
|---|---|---|
| 2,400 (15% lead rate) | ~$4,800 | ~$12,000 |
| 1,200 (30% lead rate) | ~$2,400 | ~$6,000 |

So a realistic **2-month ad budget is roughly $5k–$12k** to drive ~360 leads → ~50 funded
at $100k avg. Start with **$50–$100/day**, watch cost-per-lead in Events Manager (the Pixel
`Lead` event), kill losers, scale winners.

## The 3 multipliers that actually hit the number
1. **Speed-to-lead.** Calling/texting within 5 minutes can lift contact-to-app dramatically.
   The funnel emails you instantly — use `follow-up-templates.md` and move fast.
2. **Average loan size.** Coaching the right borrowers toward the right product (within
   compliance, subject to underwriting) moves the table above more than anything.
3. **Application completion.** The thank-you screen's "Start my application" button is why
   it's there — set `applicationUrl` so hot leads convert while they're motivated.
